What the proposed brief would cover

  • One named consumer-service competitor and one concrete comparison question.
  • A concise written profile with dated links to the public offer, pricing and important scope restrictions.
  • Where accessible, customer-review themes with counterevidence and company responses. Review availability and representativeness would be stated.
  • A clear separation between company claims, customer accounts, research inference and information that could not be verified.

This is AI-assisted desk research, not hands-on testing, customer interviews, statistical market sizing, legal or investment advice. No guaranteed review count, comprehensive market coverage, revenue forecast or delivery deadline is offered. Confidential data would not be needed for this concept.

Consumer competitor evidence sample — Yardzen
Checked 23 November 2026. Internal feasibility exercise, not commissioned work or hands-on product review. AI-assisted research.

Buyer question: Is the competing offer a standalone visual concept or a supported design-to-build service?

Observed offer (company claims, not independently tested): Signature Backyard includes backyard/side-yard designs, daytime/night renders, CAD plans and two revision rounds. Listed base price USD1,995; directly opened page displays promotional USD1,496. Extra revision USD295; expedited upgrade USD395. Local contractor visit/estimate has select-area restriction. Construction cost is not established here. Search-index price alone missed the promotion. Checkout taxes, offer expiry and eligibility unknown.
Source: https://yardzen.com/back-yard-signature

Customer evidence: Trustpilot aggregate page exposes positive and negative reports, with company replies. One September14 review alleges missed preferences, unsuitable plant choices and no contractor connection; the company says changes have since been made. Another September24 updated review reports delays but also acknowledges subsequent refund options, including a full refund. Omitting that update would distort the evidence. These are individual accounts, not established facts about typical delivery. Positive October1 and September30 reports describe satisfactory collaboration. No complaint prevalence inferred.
Source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/yardzen.com
